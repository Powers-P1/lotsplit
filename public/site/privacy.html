Prototype privacy

This is a hackathon prototype, updated 1 November 2026. Please use fictional inventory and the supplied test identities. Do not upload personal documents or enter real card details.

What the application keeps

The server stores the lot, uploaded photos, reviewed catalogue, buyer names and invitation email addresses, basket options and limits, purchase terms and consent, and payment-operation identifiers and statuses in SQLite. Buyers see their own preferences and limit; the seller can review buyer options and limits to coordinate the lot. One configured Sandbox workspace is shared by reviewers.

Sign-in and judging cookies keep the browser session open. They do not grant purchase consent. The optional rehearsal guide stores only its progress in session storage. No advertising or analytics tracker is included.

Which services are involved

  • Render hosts the application and its storage. The free rehearsal instance can lose its synthetic state on restart; the separate Sandbox instance retains its financial history.
  • Nebius processes model requests. Uploaded photos, catalogue details, buyer requests and relevant basket constraints may be sent for AI suggestions. Suggestions are reviewed before use.
  • PayPal Sandbox handles test checkout and provider operations. LOTSPLIT does not receive PayPal passwords or card details from the checkout page.
  • YouTube loads only when you choose to play the embedded film. Its privacy-enhanced player then uses its own services.

Testing access and retention

Private judging credentials and the test payer are supplied only in the Devpost testing field. Provider API keys remain on the server. Do not publish invitation links or use a real PayPal account for the demonstration.

Judging access is planned through 15 December 2026. The completed Sandbox verification ledger is retained separately from the fresh judging workspace; payment history is not erased to replay the demo. This prototype does not yet provide a self-service account or data-deletion process.

Contact

Contact the project through GitHub issues. Keep reports free of passwords, private invitation links and personal data. For confidential matters, contact the project owner through Devpost before sharing any details.
