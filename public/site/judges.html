The short route

  1. Check an alternative with AI. The assistant uses actual NVIDIA/Nebius inference and checked tools. A person decides whether to send its question.
  2. Let the buyer answer. Open Jan’s demo role and accept or refuse the complete alternative. Saving an option does not approve a purchase.
  3. Review the exact plan. Every position must be assigned once, within confirmed limits and acceptable whole baskets.
  4. See payment collection. The quick guide explicitly loads saved simulated consents and holds. Click collection yourself. It is visibly a payment rehearsal.
  5. See a buyer leave. The prepared repair shows unchanged commitments kept and changed baskets needing fresh consent and holds.

The guide is optional. Skip or close it at any point, then restart it from the app menu. Office, Garage and Café examples contain different goods, prices and group sizes. Create a lot starts an empty catalogue. The rehearsal is a shared sample workspace: loading a scenario replaces its synthetic data, without touching the separate Sandbox.

Actual PayPal Sandbox

Open the separate Sandbox workspace. Use its private seller/buyer invitations and the supplied test payer from Devpost. Every buyer approves exact purchase terms and separately authorizes PayPal. The seller can collect only after all required current holds are ready. Captures are separate payments; this is not escrow or atomic group settlement.

The shared workspace starts with reviewed inventory and basket preferences, with no purchase consents or holds pre-created. Another reviewer may already have completed it; completed history is retained. Use separate browser profiles for independent roles, or sign out before switching. Use the replay instance for repeat runs.

What the demonstration proves

The deployed integration verified real AI, separate Sandbox approvals, selective repair, four completed captures totalling 1,800 PLN, signed webhook delivery and retained financial history across restart. The film’s separate failure interlude is explicitly simulated; actual injected-failure/refund evidence is an earlier isolated local run.

Goods and buyer roles are fictional. One test payer represents the buyers. Production payments, buyer discovery, identity verification, shipping, real customer adoption and physical handover are outside the prototype. The guide never authorizes or captures actual Sandbox funds. Read the prototype privacy notice.
