# Demo asset provenance

The catalog assets are original AI-generated visuals created for LOTSPLIT. All six examples depict fictional inventory. The mixed garage-sale bundle, small-company office assets, workshop equipment, records, books and illustrations are prepared demonstration data; their descriptions, condition notes and fixed shares do not document real goods inspected or offered by a seller.

| Example | Asset paths and use |
| --- | --- |
| **Garage Sale** | `public/assets/examples/home/collection.png` is the overview; `atlas.png` supplies 24 item tiles for the mixed bundle, including a bicycle, instruments, tools, electronics, home and sports goods. The `home` identifier is historical; this is a mixed garage-sale lot. |
| **Small Business Clearance** | `public/assets/examples/office/collection.png` is the overview; `atlas.png` supplies 24 fictional computers, furniture, printer, network and office/back-room equipment tiles. It depicts a whole asset bundle, not a real company's sale or a billing/invoice workflow. |
| **Workshop Equipment** | `public/assets/examples/workshop/collection.png` is the overview; `atlas.png` supplies 24 power-tool, hand-tool and accessory tiles. |
| **Second-Hand Vinyl** | `public/assets/examples/vinyl/collection.png` is the overview; `atlas.png` contains 24 separate record-cover tiles used for the item images. |
| **Moving-Day Bookshelf** | `public/assets/examples/books/collection.png` is the overview; `atlas.png` contains 24 separate book-cover tiles used for the item images. |
| **Collectible Illustrations** | `public/assets/collection.png` is the original overview; `public/assets/items/01.png` through `24.png` are 24 separate illustrated-card images. |

Each of the five atlases contains 24 tiles rendered as matching item thumbnails. An atlas is an asset format, not a photograph of an inspected lot. No item image establishes the actual condition, authenticity or value of physical goods. The retained illustration set has 24 separate item PNGs. Unused generation variants and prompts are working evidence and are not part of the source package.

These assets accompany the project under its [MIT license](../LICENSE). No external seller photographs, commercial album/book cover images or card-franchise artwork are used as catalog assets. Installed libraries, fonts and icons retain their upstream licenses.

Showing these assets in a seeded catalog does not establish that AI read a supplied photo. Live Nebius text preferences and a live vision request are recorded in the [verification record](verification.md). The vision request processed only the original `public/assets/items/01.png` and returned an unconfirmed proposal; it did not generate any complete 24-item catalog. None of the added examples carries a separate live vision or PayPal purchase claim.
