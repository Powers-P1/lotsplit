# Internal review and follow-up — 30 October 2026

The same three Codex jurors who reviewed the concept inspected the local MVP in their existing chats. This was an internal tuning review by the same panel, not a new blind evaluation, an official organizer verdict or customer validation. It did not produce a new numerical score. The jurors reviewed documentation, selected source paths and desktop/mobile screenshots; they did not repeat the automated tests or provider transactions.

| Juror perspective | Recorded conclusion |
| --- | --- |
| Buyer and business | GO for the local MVP, conditional GO for the final competition demonstration. Fixed shares, explicit alternatives, exact consent and the packing gate match the accepted concept. |
| Systems and settlement | Conditional GO. The inspected allocation, versioned consent, payment operation records and reconciliation gates showed no blocking contradiction with the accepted model. Integration verification remains incomplete. |
| Competition and UX | Conditional GO for the final demonstration. The reviewed desktop/mobile screens and consent/payment boundaries fit the concept, while the recording still needs a complete, coherent story. |

The shared requirements were to verify the complete buyer interface through genuine PayPal approval and return to the correct application session; verify actual Sandbox failure handling, including refund, void, provider readbacks and webhook delivery/signatures; and add complete used-vinyl and book examples. The UX review also asked for one visible chain from an AI proposal through human review and application to allocation, consent and payment.

Six rehearsal examples now broaden the demonstration through their own catalog content and the same allocation, consent, replanning and packing process. The default mixed garage-sale lot, fictional small-company office assets and workshop equipment show different object types, prices and budgets; vinyl, books and illustrations are retained. The office example uses the same bundle-purchase mechanism and adds no billing, invoice or company-liquidation system. This follows later user steering as well as the panel's original breadth request. Selection resets only rehearsal data. These additions do not complete the remaining live integration checks, and the earlier panel verdicts predate the added examples. The panel has not issued a new verdict on the expanded build.

The recorded live proof remains four logical buyers in separate application sessions using one Sandbox payer account, four completed captures totaling PLN 840, and a separately verified single-image vision proposal. The [verification record](verification.md) preserves those boundaries. Public source publication, an English YouTube demonstration, judge access and Devpost submission remain delivery work.
